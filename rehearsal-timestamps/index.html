    What can I type?
    
    
      
  • Times like 19:05, 7:05, 7:05pm, 19.05 or 19:05:30 all work.
    • 
      
  • Extra punctuation is fine: 19:05: Warm-ups and 19:05 - Warm-ups give the same result.
    • 
      
  • 21: on its own means 21:00.
    • 
      
  • Lines that don't start with a time are copied over unchanged.
    • 
      
  • Rehearsals that run past midnight are handled.
